# 2. Current Design (single source of truth)
Status: this section describes the live game at v5 (commit c6f4141). The v6 overhaul (section 4) is being built on top of it; when v6 ships, this section is rewritten to match and the differences are recorded in the Decision Log (section 6) and Version History (section 7).
## 2.1 The loop
• Title, then Captain select (pick one of four captains, optionally a Notoriety level), then the Act I map.
• On the map you pick the next node (forward or diagonal). Each leg eats one ration. Nodes: fight, elite, event, port, shipwright, recruit, careening cove, treasure, boss.
• Fights give silver, cargo, a card pick and sometimes a prize ship or officer. Damage carries over, so every fight is a spend of hull, crew and captain HP.
• After each act boss you win a trophy and may take your plunder and sail home (bank 75%) or press on. Dying banks 25%; winning Act III banks 125%.
## 2.2 Captains
• Blackbeard (78 HP, 34 crew): Terror, enemy crews start boarding with 10% fewer men. Signature card Lit Fuses in the Beard. Intimidation and boarding.
• Bellamy (66 HP, 40 crew): Prince of Pirates, starts with Henry Jennings as Quartermaster, +30% silver from fights, hires cost 25% less. Signature card Prince's Share. Crew and plunder.
• Vane (62 HP, 34 crew): No quarter, cannon cards +2 damage and +3 gunpowder at the start. Signature card Defiance. Aggressive gunnery.
• Bonny and Read (66 HP, 34 crew): Two blades, Captain cards deal +2 damage and +1 energy in duels. Signature card Twin Blades. Hand-to-hand and duels.
• Rackham (68 HP, unlock after the first extraction or win): sail cards give +3 Block and he can flee normal fights on turn 1. Signature card Fast Getaway.
• Every captain starts with a 10-card deck, a sloop (45 hull, 40 crew cap), 60 silver, 5 powder, 6 rations and 1 repair kit, and no officers (except Bellamy's Jennings).
## 2.3 Combat
• Turn: 3 energy, draw 5 (one extra on turn 1 in fair wind or with Hornigold's Spyglass). Tap a card to select it (the full text and numbers appear), tap Play to confirm. End Turn is at the bottom.
• Two phases from one deck. Ship phase: only Ship and Captain cards are playable. Boarding phase: only Crew and Captain cards are playable. A card that is not playable can be swapped for a fresh draw once per turn.
• Ship phase: cannon cards hit the enemy hull. Gunpowder fuels cannon cards (no powder halves the damage). Block absorbs the enemy's attacks that turn. Weak cuts damage by 25%. Burn deals damage each turn. Advantage tokens (max 3) give extra cards and energy when boarding starts.
• When the enemy hull is at 50% or less in a normal fight you choose: board her (full loot, a chance at a prize ship and an officer) or keep firing until she sinks (about 40% loot, no boarding risk). Bosses are scripted.
• Carry-over into boarding: enemy hull at 50% or less shakes their crew (-15%), at 25% or less (-30%). A clean approach gives +1 Advantage, a battered hull gives -1. Heavy hull hits kill your crew (1 per 4 damage), aimed shots can injure an officer.
• Boarding: your crew fights on its own each turn (1 enemy crew per 7 of yours) while your cards kill more. The enemy cycles melee, melee, rush the captain, hunt officers, brace. At 0 enemy crew you win. You can cut the grapples in a normal fight (-20% crew, no loot).
• Duel (Hornigold only): captain against captain, with a cycle of slash, slash, guard, lunge.
• Intents: every enemy move is shown as text and an icon with the number and target (hull, crew, captain), with the enemy's whole pattern as a small strip.
• Weather is rolled per fight and shown first: Fair wind (+1 card on turn 1), Weather Gauge yours (first cannon card each turn +3), Weather Gauge theirs (enemy broadsides +2), Squall (all cannon fire -2 for both sides).
• Loss states: hull at 0 means Abandon Ship (drop to a periagua, lose 40% crew, half the cargo and silver, an officer is injured, the run goes on). Captain at 0 ends the run. Losing your ship at a boss sends you back two nodes once; a second loss means capture.
## 2.4 Cards (32)
• 15 Ship cards: Broadside, Swivel Gun, Chain Shot, Grapeshot, Raking Fire, Tack Hard, Crowd Sail, Take the Weather Gauge, Fireship, plus officer cards (All Hands to Pumps, Splice the Line, Double-Shotted, Heated Shot, Read the Wind, Shoal Run).
• 8 Crew cards: Cutlasses, Pistol Volley, Boarding Axes, Hold the Rail, Rally the Crew, Grenadoes, Fearsome Reputation, Fair Shares (officer).
• 9 Captain cards: Parry, Captain's Pistol, Iron Resolve, Ship's Articles (officer), and the five captain signatures.
• Costs: 22 cards cost 1, 5 cost 0, 5 cost 2. Fourteen cards involve Block. Keywords: Block, Weak, Burn, Advantage, Exhaust, powder pips.
• Rewards pick 1 of 3 from a pool of 17 shared cards (elites give two picks). The port sells cards and removal.
• Known weakness: a Ship card is dead in boarding and a Crew card is dead at sea, so a hand can have two or three unplayable draws (Critique R2-3).
## 2.5 Officers
• Four roles, one officer per role, two candidates each: Quartermaster (Henry Jennings, a hard-bargaining purser), Bosun (Tom Pruitt, Barty Shea), Master Gunner (Israel Hands, Abel Hatch), Navigator (Matthias Penhallow, a Spanish pilot).
• Each brings a signature card (added to your deck while he is fit) and a passive. They are earned at Recruit nodes (shown with the role on the map), events and boarded ships, never given for free.
• An injured officer's card and passive are off until a cove rest or the port surgeon.
• Known weakness: the plan called for Surgeon and Boarding Master roles; they were never built.
## 2.6 Hull, crew, captain and supplies
• Three persistent tracks: Hull (ship), Crew (a number up to the ship's cap), Captain HP.
• Supplies: Gunpowder (cap 14, 8 silver, fuels cannon cards), Rations (cap 12, 4 silver, one eaten per leg, 4 crew starve per leg if none), Repair kits (cap 6, 10 silver, patch the hull at coves and with Bosun cards). Silver is the only money.
• Healing is never automatic: hull at a cove (30% plus kits), captain and officers at a cove rest (30% HP) or the port surgeon, crew by hiring (3 silver each) or Rally cards.
## 2.7 Ships
• Periagua (30 hull, 25 crew, 2 guns): +3 Block each ship turn.
• Sloop (45, 40, 3 guns, start ship, 140): first sail card each fight is free.
• Brigantine (65, 60, 4 guns, 300): +1 card on boarding.
• Snow (85, 80, 5 guns, 500): cannon cards +2 damage.
• Frigate (110, 110, 6 guns, 800): enemy crews start -10%.
• Three upgrades, two levels each (70 and 140 silver): Sheathing (+15 hull), Heavier guns (+1 guns), New rigging (+3 Block per ship turn). Trade-in = half base price times hull condition plus half of upgrade spend. Boarded ships can be taken as prizes.
## 2.8 Map and nodes
• Three lanes wide, forward or one lane diagonal, 10 / 7 / 7 rows in the three acts plus the boss row. Row 0 is always a fight, the middle row is treasure, the last row is a careening cove.
• Weights: fight 42, event 20, recruit 10, port 9, elite 8 (14 at Notoriety 1 or more), shipwright 4. Guaranteed recruits on rows 1 and just after the middle, a port by row 2. Elite, recruit, port and shipwright never repeat down a lane.
• Cove: choose one of repair the hull or rest (heal the captain and officers). Port: buy supplies and cards, remove a card (50 silver +25 each), carpenter, surgeon, hire crew. Shipwright: upgrade, buy or trade in a ship.
• Events: 12, each with 2 or 3 risky options and stated odds (drifting wreck, 1715 fleet wrecks, storm, officer without a ship, smugglers, fishing village, plate fleet straggler, fever, powder hulk, King's Pardon, informant, press gang).
## 2.9 Acts and bosses
• Act I, The Flying Gang (10 rows, scale 1.0): rival pirates, merchants, privateers, wreckers; elite: Navy Patrol Frigate. Boss: Pirate-Hunter Squadron, a two-ship fight (an escort sloop, then the flagship you must board). Trophy: Commodore's Pennant (+8 max hull).
• Act II, The Treasure Coast (7 rows, scale 1.3): Spanish Guarda Costa, privateers, galleons; elite: Capitana. Boss: the Guarda Costa Stockade, a crew assault on foot (58 crew). Trophy: Letter of Marque (ports 15% cheaper).
• Act III, The Hunter (7 rows, scale 1.55): hunters, cutters, guarda costas; elite: Royal Navy Warship. Boss: Benjamin Hornigold in three stages: his Ranger (85 hull), boarding (55 crew), then a captain duel. Trophy: Hornigold's Spyglass (+1 card on the first turn of every fight).
• 15 enemy types, each with a fixed intent loop shuffled at fight start, damage scaled by act, row and Notoriety.
## 2.10 Extraction, payouts and score
• Extraction points are after each act boss (not anywhere else). Take your plunder and sail home: bank 75% of your silver, keep the trophies. Death: bank 25%, lose the trophies. Win Act III: bank 125%. All payouts are multiplied by (1 + 0.1 x Notoriety).
## 2.11 Meta progression and Nassau
• Banked silver builds the Nassau base: Shipyard (150 / 400), Powder House (120 / 300), Harbour Tavern (120 / 300), each with two levels that give starting bonuses.
• Notoriety ladder, 0 to 5 (each win unlocks the next): Hunted (more elites), Hardened crews (+15% enemy crews), Gouging merchants (+20% prices), Heavy guns (+15% enemy hulls), Admiral's revenge (+20% bosses).
• Logbook: runs, wins, deaths, extractions, ships sunk, boardings, best act, trophies and per-captain record. Rackham unlocks after the first extraction or win.
## 2.12 Look, feel and platform
• The chart-table look: a captain's table with a leather logbook HUD, a pinned sea chart for the map and a brass-and-parchment UI, with painted storybook art (captains, enemy ships, Nassau, Hornigold) and SVG fallbacks.
• Procedural WebAudio sound effects (no music), a mute toggle, a Reduce motion toggle. Layout is portrait 390x844 with no page scrolling in combat and on the map, and respects safe areas.
• Saves in localStorage (run, meta, mute, reduce-motion) and auto-saves at every beat. No tutorial, no settings screen, no offline support.
## 2.13 Balance snapshot (v5, measured with the bot simulator)
• 240 bot runs win about 29%. A bot that never takes a card wins 23%. A bot that always takes the middle lane wins 24%. So deck building adds only about six points, and ship tier and route add little: choices do not yet matter enough (Critiques R2-2 and R2-5).
• About 60% of lost runs end at Hornigold in Act III, so Acts I and II rarely decide the run (Critique R2-1).
• A bot run is about 13.6 fights and 549 actions; earlier human-style playtests measured a median of about 820 taps for a full run (Critique R2-6).
## 2.14 Tech

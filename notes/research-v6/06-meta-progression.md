## What they say
- Hades: Mirror of Night talents are bought with Darkness and are swappable between runs for free (12 pairs, red or green side active per run). "Heat" is a menu of difficulty conditions that pay out the rarest currencies, so mastery has a visible, self-tuned ladder. Every run, win or lose, advances story and currency, which removes the sting of dying (Game Developer).
- StS: the roster is locked to one class at start, each class unlocks by clearing a run, per-character card/relic pools unlock in waves of three, and Ascension 1 appears only after a first Act 3 kill. The death screen carries three hooks: locked-roster silhouettes, "next run's pool is literally different", and the difficulty ladder as a reward.
- Monster Train: choose two clans (5 choose 2) and a champion variant, "20 possible ways to start each run", which fights repetition. Covenant ranks (search summary) escalate difficulty for mastery.
- Vampire Survivors: gold persists even after death and buys permanent power-ups, so every run advances you; evolutions reward learning combos across runs (search summary).
- Across the Obelisk and Pirates Outlaws: persistent progression makes later runs stronger but critics point at grind (slow, grindy progression; battles that drag; multi-hour runs).
- Dicey Dungeons: each class varies a fundamental rule rather than just numbers. Cavanagh also found that when picking up cards is a pure cost, the best strategy is to "avoid everything": he added "trash" cards and constraints so decks need friction and trade-offs.
- Loop Hero's resource retention (100% retreat at camp, 60% mid-loop, 30% death per the Game Design Lab summary) is the same bank-or-risk shape we use.

## Takeaways for Free Nassau
1. Every voyage (win, sail home, or sunk) must bank something visible and reasonably large: a "Captain's Log" run summary with silver banked, Notoriety earned, and "Nassau progress: +N". Fix the average ~30 silver on a death by banking Renown (notoriety points) per node, boss, and elite, plus 25% of plunder.
2. Meta spend must change options, not just add stats: Nassau buildings unlock new starting doctrines, officer slots, new nodes, and new cards in the reward pool (like StS card waves), with a locked silhouette visible from the start.
3. Unlock chain: locked decision says all four launch captains (Blackbeard, Bellamy, Vane, Bonny & Read) are playable from the start, so we keep that. Rackham stays an unlock (first win). New unlocks: Doctrines (alternate starter decks), more cards in the reward pool, officers, node types, and Flags (a Heat-style modifier menu).
4. Notoriety becomes a "Black Flag" Heat menu: pick modifiers (more elites, fewer rations, stronger bosses, mutiny risk) each worth Heat; tiers N1-N5 unlock titles on the Nassau wall.
5. Two-clan-style variety: choose a Doctrine (Gunnery / Boarding / Seamanship / Cunning) as a second pool at run start, so each run starts differently (4 captains x 3 doctrines = 12 starts).
